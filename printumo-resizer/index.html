Printumo Print Resizer

Turn one high-resolution image into print-ready files for every Printumo paper size

1 Choose your picture

Drop an image here or tap to browse — JPEG, PNG, WebP. Nothing is uploaded; it all happens in your browser.

2 Check the crop and settings

Drag on the picture to move the focal point — crops keep that spot centred. The orange box shows the crop for the highlighted size below.

With upscaling off, a print larger than your image allows is still produced at the best size the file supports — it just lands below the target DPI, which the badges tell you.

3 Pick the print sizes

Badges compare the pixels available after cropping against the selected DPI: Excellent meets it, Acceptable is 100–150 DPI, Too small is under 100 DPI and will look soft in print.

4 Make the files

About the sizes

Sizes follow Printumo's fine art paper print catalogue, and every rectangular size is offered in both portrait and landscape. Printumo's guidance is 150 DPI for paper prints, which is what this tool targets by default; output files carry the correct DPI tag so the print stage reads them at the intended size. If Printumo changes its catalogue, edit the PRINTUMO_SIZES list near the top of this file's script.

Runs entirely offline in your browser — no image ever leaves this device.
